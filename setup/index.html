Set up FireCasa

Fill in the boxes, then press the buttons. Everything runs on this computer.

1. Your Fire TV

On the TV: Settings > My Fire TV > About > Network shows its address. Then turn on Developer Options > ADB debugging.

2. What this computer needs

FireCasa needs Android platform tools to talk to the TV. Download the zip from developer.android.com, unzip it, and reload this page after putting its folder where you can find it. Mac with Homebrew: brew install --cask android-platform-tools does the same.

Java and the Android SDK are only needed if you want to build your own copy. Otherwise FireCasa downloads the ready-made app.

3. Keys for movie and show info

These are optional. Without them, the matching rows and photos just don't show. Each one is free. Paste it in and press Save, or leave a box blank to keep what is saved.

Get one at themoviedb.org/settings/api. Copy the "API Read Access Token", the long one.

Get one at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx. Used for the IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores.

Get one at unsplash.com/developers. Gives the rotating background photos.

Keys are sent to your TV. They are never built into the app, so the app you install has none of yours in it.

4. Clean up Amazon's extras

Turns off ads, tracking and background junk. Apps are switched off, never deleted, and you can undo it.

Always included: ads, tracking, telemetry, shopping, News and demo mode.

5. Install FireCasa

Puts FireCasa on the TV and sends it your keys. If this computer can't build the app, it downloads the ready-made one. When you press this, look at the TV and choose Always allow if it asks.

Undo

Turns Amazon's apps and home screen back on.
